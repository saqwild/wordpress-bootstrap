# YouTube Channel Benchmark — "Explained From Within"

Working notes for validating the channel strategy against comparable channels.
Data is collected manually from YouTube (the research environment cannot reach youtube.com).

## Hypothesis under test

> A **two-layer story** — a relatable human situation *outside* the body, cut with
> the explanation *inside* the body — outperforms pure explanation, and 3D/AI visuals
> alone are not enough.

If story channels outperform no-story channels of similar size and style, the hypothesis holds.

## Data to collect per channel

1. About: subscribers, video count, total views, join date, country
2. Last 10 videos: title, views, length, age
3. Top 5 (Popular): title, views, age
4. First 60 seconds of a top video: human story first, or straight to explanation?
