## Reading

**Resolves the length confounder: long-form alone does not work.**
- Same length as Think Science (11–15 min), same niche, 3D — but textbook "What is [disease]?" /
  "Anatomy of [organ]" titles and no story → ~720-view median. Identical ratio to Nucleus (~0.004×).
- Nucleus (short, no story) and Inside Us (long, no story) fail equally → **length is not the engine**.
- Its best videos are **study compilations** (1-hour, 24-hour anatomy) — search-driven student audience,
  not recommendation-driven curious adults.
- Story and "you" title framing always co-occur in the winners; they can't be separated with this data.
  Treat them as **one package**.

## Lessons for "Explained From Within"

1. Length supports a story; it doesn't replace one.
2. Avoid textbook framing ("What is X?", "Anatomy of X") — it targets students searching, not viewers browsing.
3. Later asset: once 10+ episodes exist, a compilation of our own episodes is a legitimate long TV-viewing format
   (also seen on The Infographics Show, 15M) — add new framing/intro so it isn't a bare re-upload.
