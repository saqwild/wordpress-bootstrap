## Opening (observed)

  (the brain's arterial blood supply), then defines the condition.
- Textbook / patient-leaflet tone.

## Reading

**Supports the hypothesis, with confounders.**
- Same visual category as us (3D, inside the body), far higher accuracy and a 28× larger
  subscriber base — yet recent median views are ~18× lower than Think Science.
  → Accurate 3D alone does not earn recommendations in 2026.
- Confounders (cannot be separated using this channel alone):
  1. Clinical, disease/procedure titles (patient search intent) vs "you + action + time" curiosity titles.
  2. 3–6 min vs 10–15 min (less watch time per view).
  3. B2B business — the channel is not optimised for YouTube growth.
  4. Old, partly inactive subscriber base.
- Their single biggest video (340M) uses the **"What Happens If You…"** framing — consistent
  with the title pattern found elsewhere.
- Their other giants are **universal, evergreen, searchable** topics (conception, childbirth) —
  a long-tail search asset, not a recommendation hit.

## Lessons for "Explained From Within"

1. Accuracy is for **trust and defensibility**, not a growth engine by itself.
2. Don't compete with medical studios on anatomical precision (AI video can't match them);
   compete on **story, framing and length**.
3. Keep a small share of **evergreen, searchable** "What happens if you…" topics — they compound for years.
