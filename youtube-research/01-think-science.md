## Opening / storytelling (observed by viewing)

- Opens with a **second-person story scenario**: "you" do something (work a long day,
  run X miles, skip a meal), then "what happens" — the viewer is the protagonist.
- Visuals show a **relatable person in real life first**, then zoom into the body.
- Narration hooks immediately (reframe line in the first ~10 s) while the visual story
  builds in parallel — story does not delay the hook.
- Master metaphor set up early (e.g. "warehouse"), paid off at ~45% of runtime.

## Accuracy notes

Top hits include debunked or overstated claims (blood-type diet, eye-color "advantages",
"gut detox", "autophagy = fountain of youth", "muscle loss is a myth").
Structure is worth copying; claims are not. See claim ledger in strategy notes.
